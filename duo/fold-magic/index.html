Rehearse, then fold to reveal

OverviewPrivacySupport

Fold the performance screen into a reveal cue, after privately preparing the trick.

A symbol on folded paper matches a sealed screen. Three dice hide a total you already know. TrickPane pairs everyday props with a quiet audience display for small, prepared surprises.

Learn ten card, symbol and number routines through private rehearsal notes. Prepare the effect, then switch to the large audience display. On an ordinary smartphone, hold the upper left corner to reveal and the upper right to finish. The spectator never needs to touch the screen. Bring physical cards, paper or dice for routines that use them.

Record up to 30 seconds of the performance screen, optionally include your voice, and review the clip before sharing.

Three routines are free without play limits. The one-time Magic Pack (JPY700/USD4.99; your local store price applies) adds seven routines and removes all ads. Free users may see ads after performances, or choose a rewarded video for one performance of a paid trick. Core tricks work offline; purchases and ads require a connection.
