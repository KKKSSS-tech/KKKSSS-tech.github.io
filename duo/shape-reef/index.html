CornerZoo

Two shapes become an animal

OverviewPrivacySupport

Find an animal where two shapes meet on a folded-paper model.

CornerZoo joins bold shapes into a fox, rabbit, elephant, turtle, penguin or cat. Explore freely, follow a picture or try a short clue. Turn the virtual viewpoint and choose an ink to see how the pieces fit. Keep up to six animals in your on-device field guide.

There are no timed challenges or movement scores. A grown-up can set a play reminder and erase the guide after confirmation.

On a non-folding iPhone, use the same virtual fold and viewpoint controls. This version uses a screen model; it does not support physical two-display perspective illusions.

No ads, in-app purchases or external transmission of app data.
