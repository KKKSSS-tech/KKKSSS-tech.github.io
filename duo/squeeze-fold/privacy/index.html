Sound Sketchbook — eight-bar editor

Updated November 17, 2026

Sound Sketchbook stores your compositions and processes purchase-verification results on your device. The app includes no advertising or analytics SDK.

Your compositions

The editor keeps a working song and named saves on your device. They contain the song title, an identifier, tempo, editing position, update time, and chord and percussion events with their positions, lengths and strengths. The app generates playback audio from those events on the device. It does not use a microphone or camera to create music.

The versioned song file is stored in the app's Application Support directory. Device or system backups may include this file. The editor retains your notes when a save fails and offers a retry. When an existing file cannot be read or has an unsupported version, the editor protects it from replacement. Older instrument data remains separate from the composition file.

Purchases

The studio uses StoreKit to check, purchase and restore the existing Full Bellows nonconsumable for this app. StoreKit provides its localized price and verified transaction information. The app does not request or store payment card details. Basic six-chord editing, percussion and saving are free. Full Bellows adds the Dusk and Drift progressions and the B-flat-major and D-major chord pads. Saved notes remain playable and editable when verification is unavailable or a purchase is revoked. Purchases from the separate ClapCase app are not transferred or queried.

Verified purchase information is processed on the device to enable purchased features. Apple handles payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. App Store and Privacy

Data collection and tracking

The app processes compositions and purchase-verification results on the device. It has no developer-operated upload, account, cloud-sync or analytics service, advertising identifier access, or cross-app tracking feature. Apple services and system backups operate according to their own policies and your system settings.

Keeping and removing data

The editor has no destructive reset or song-deletion control in this version. Removing the app through system settings removes its local app data. Backups are managed separately through system and backup settings. Do not remove the app as a troubleshooting step while trying to recover an unreadable song file.

Support and public pages

Support messages contain the information you choose to send. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com with a description of the issue. We use information in your support message to respond and troubleshoot. Avoid sending credentials, payment details, or songs containing information you do not want to share.

Public support pages use GitHub Pages.

Earlier Squeezefold — bellows instrument

Updated: October 1, 2026

App data

Progression, voicing and sound preferences are saved in a small local settings file that system backups may include. Touch and hinge readings are processed locally. No microphone, camera, recording, music download, account or sharing is used.

Advertising and choices

Advertising uses Google Mobile Ads and consent management. For ad delivery, measurement and improvement, the SDK may collect IP addresses (which may estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, crash and performance data, ads viewed and product interactions. See Google's data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Regional consent is checked before ad initialization. Tracking follows your system permission, and you can play without granting it. Personalization is restricted when the necessary consent or tracking permission is absent; this does not eliminate all ad SDK data processing. Revisit consent through the app's advertising privacy options when required, and change tracking permission in system settings. A verified ad-removal purchase stops ad requests. Google manages its retention, deletion and international processing under its policies.

Purchases

StoreKit contacts Apple for products, purchases and restoration. The app uses verified purchase entitlements. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment details. Apple manages transaction records and refunds.

Storage and deletion

Manage local settings and records using the app's deletion controls or by removing the app. Keychain credentials, shared copies, device backups and provider records may require separate management.

Public pages and support

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics or external fonts; the host may process access information such as IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your reply address and message are used to handle the inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com for support, privacy questions or requests about inquiry information, including deletion.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
