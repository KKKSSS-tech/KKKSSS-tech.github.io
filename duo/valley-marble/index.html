ValleyRoll

Marble mazes and custom paths

OverviewPrivacySupport

Roll through a fold-shaped valley, slowing at the bends before the next green goal.

A brass marble. A deep blue valley. One mint-green goal. Ease around a corner, control your momentum, and find a faster way through.

Explore 30 distinct mazes and keep your best finishing times. Tilt your device to steer, or hold the on-screen arrows. Saved play lets you return to the same position after a break.

Want a path of your own? Add and remove walls in a 9-by-13 course editor. ValleyRoll checks that the goal is reachable before saving your creation on your device.

The first five stages and course creation are free. Free play may show ads between courses. An optional rewarded video grants one play of a locked stage. A one-time purchase unlocks all 30 stages and removes ads; pricing varies by storefront.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Purchases and ads need an internet connection. Saved courses can be played offline.

A non-folding iPhone gives you the same mazes and editor: steer by device tilt or the arrow buttons.
