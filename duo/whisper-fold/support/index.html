Getting started

Choose a story, open the curtain with touch, then read or choose Listen. Stop ends speech. On a non-folding phone, covering it can start speech; uncovering it stops speech started this way. Proximity state is handled on the device and is not saved. If a voice is unavailable, use text reading. Removing the app removes its local shelf; manage system backups separately. Restore a full-story purchase in the purchase controls when available.
