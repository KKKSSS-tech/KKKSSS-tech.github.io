Getting started

Before recording, explain its purpose to participants, inform them that audio will be recorded and get their agreement. Permit microphone access, check supported speech language assets, then start a meeting. Keep the app in the foreground. Tap a timestamp to replay audio; saved recordings cannot be appended to. Delete meetings in the archive to remove their local audio and text. Check for confidential information before exporting Markdown. Exported files and device backups are managed by their chosen providers.
