PopcornFold

Find the heat, hear the pop

OverviewPrivacySupport

Fold the pan to change the heat, and listen for the first kernel to pop.

A pan full of quiet kernels. A little warmth. Then the first pop.

Rub the pan to build heat, ease off before the popcorn scorches, and serve when the batch feels right. Watch the kernels jump and compare the unburnt popcorn with your best batch. Sound can be switched off.

Play as many standard batches as you like for free. An optional rewarded video lets you replay the same arrangement. A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks those replays. Prices are shown by the App Store in your local currency.

PopcornFold works on ordinary touch-screen phones. Fold-angle heat control depends on compatible hardware and platform support. This is a game, never a guide to cooking.
