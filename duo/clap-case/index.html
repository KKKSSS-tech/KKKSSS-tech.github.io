ClapCase

A little stage for applause

OverviewPrivacySupport

A gentle fold brings two illustrated hands together in applause. ClapCase gives a friend's performance a tiny curtain call.

Choose classic claps, a deliberately slow clap or an ovation, with Bravo, Wow and Encore reactions. Sound and haptics give the gesture weight; turn sound off and keep the hands and reactions visible.

Leave a pause between gestures. There is no clap counter or speed competition.

On a non-folding iPhone, swipe up or down to clap. Classic applause and the reactions are free with ads at session end. An optional rewarded ad opens the styles for that session; a one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks all styles.
