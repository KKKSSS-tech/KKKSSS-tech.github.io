TripSide

Your itinerary beside tickets

OverviewPrivacySupport

Unfold a travel folio that keeps your itinerary beside its documents.

Create a trip, add dated plans with time zones, places and notes, then attach locally copied images, PDFs or signed passes. Select a plan to see its document. A wide layout shows timeline and detail together; compact layouts start with the next upcoming item.

On a non-folding iPhone, the next-item home and itinerary controls give access to the same plans and documents. Documents are available offline after import. Selecting a cloud file may first require its provider to download it.

One trip is free. A one-time Pro unlock allows additional trips; existing trips stay readable and editable if Pro becomes unavailable. No booking, email access, ticket refresh or Wallet import is provided.

Trip data stays in an encrypted local archive excluded from device backup. Keep your original tickets: there is no archive export, migration or recovery service. No ads, accounts, cloud sync or sharing.
