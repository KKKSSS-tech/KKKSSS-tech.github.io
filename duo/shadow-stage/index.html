View ShadowStage Puppets on the App Store (paid app)

Build a folded-paper shadow stage with a virtual lamp, wall and floor.

Place and resize up to six paper puppets from six original silhouettes. Change the model wall angle and watch the shadows fall across the receivers. Follow eight dotted silhouette targets or make a stage of your own. Save up to twelve stages locally.

There is no timer or movement score: different arrangements can make the same shadow. The app is silent.

On a non-folding iPhone, use the screen controls to adjust the virtual wall and puppets. This version uses a mathematical folded model; live physical two-panel play is unavailable. The model's angles are not instructions for bending a real device.

Grown-up settings use device-owner authentication and include deletion controls. No ads, in-app purchases or external transmission of app data.
