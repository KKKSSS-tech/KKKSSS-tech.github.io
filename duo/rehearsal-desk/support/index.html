Getting started

Create a script and set paragraph times. Camera preparation asks for camera and microphone permission; you can rehearse without recording. Deleting a script retains its snapshots in history. Deleting a history removes its video too. Manage or restore Pro in the app; subscriptions can also be managed in your Apple account.
