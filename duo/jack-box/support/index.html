Getting started

Choose an effect and message, then arm it and gently close the device. On a non-folding phone, tap to trigger. Do not use it with people who startle easily, are driving or have their hands occupied. Photography is optional and off by default; show the photo to the person and let them Keep or Delete it. Saved Photos copies must be deleted in Photos. Restore purchases in the app.
