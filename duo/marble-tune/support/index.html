Getting started

Arrange pipes and the virtual model, then press Play to hear the connected route. Use the on-screen controls on a non-folding phone. Sound starts muted. Saved compositions can be deleted in the app. Grown-up settings include optional 5, 10 or 15 minute sessions; the app does not change the system volume.
