Keep a group decision together as the fold layout changes, then reveal everyone's choices at once.

Pass one device around the group. Each person answers behind a handover cover; once all answers are ready, reveal the round together. Choose planning poker, amounts, voting or a two-choice prompt you write yourself.

On a non-folding iPhone, the same pass-and-answer controls work in one pane. Fold-aware layout changes keep the session in that pane; this version does not offer simultaneous input on two physical displays. Keep the screen covered between turns: the cover is a courtesy, not secure protection from someone watching nearby.

Two to four participants are free. A one-time group unlock adds five to twelve. Up to 200 completed rounds stay in local history, which you can erase. Unrevealed answers stay in memory and are discarded when you end the round or close the process.

No ads or accounts. Two-choice prompts are conversation games, not psychological diagnoses.
