A gentle fold catches the insect at the line; your timing decides the hit.

Catch mosquitoes and flies as they cross the dotted line. Spare the bees and test your accuracy in 30-second rounds. On a standard screen, tap both sides with two fingers or use Swat.

Explore three fields: score 60 in one to unlock the next. The first field is always free. Record up to 15 seconds, review your clip, then choose where to share it.

Free play includes ads between rounds and optional videos for one trial of a locked field. A one-time purchase removes all ads and opens every field. Close gently: force and hinge speed never earn extra points.
