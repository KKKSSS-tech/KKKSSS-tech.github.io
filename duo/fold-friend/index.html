A folded landscape becomes a small home for cloth friends and their changing expressions.

Moss, Pebble and Fern live in a little paper landscape. Meet their curious, dreamy, cheerful and settled faces, then keep the ones you like.

On a regular phone, move the virtual habitat to explore every expression. Settle together with a single tap. There is no timer, hunger, streak or daily chore.

Keep four kinds of expression to meet Fern for free. Make a picture of your friend and choose where to share it. Your collection is stored on your device.

The free experience includes Moss and Pebble. A single purchase unlocks every friend and expression.
