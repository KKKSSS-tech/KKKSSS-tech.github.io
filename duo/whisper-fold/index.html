WhisperFold

Small stories behind a curtain

OverviewPrivacySupport

A fold-inspired paper nook opens onto three creatures and six short stories.

Meet a moth, snail and owl in stories about leaves, clouds and stones. Read at your own pace, or choose Listen for locally synthesized speech and Stop when you want quiet. Your local shelf remembers the stories you have read or heard and your place in the current one.

On a non-folding iPhone, touch the on-screen curtain and use Listen and Stop. You can also cover the phone to start speech and uncover it to stop speech started this way. Proximity input is handled on the device and its state is not saved. Live physical fold control is unavailable.

Three stories are free. A one-time full-story unlock is offered when its purchase is available. Ads and rewarded story trials are disabled in this version. There is no recording, sharing, microphone, account or streak requirement.
