PinchFace

A mirror with a gentle squeeze

OverviewPrivacySupport

Close the fold to squash your live mirror image a little more.

PinchFace processes the camera view on your device with five playful warps. Flat and stretch are free; an optional rewarded trial lets you try a locked warp, and a one-time unlock adds all five and removes ads. Ads do not interrupt the mirror, recording or clip preview.

On a non-folding iPhone, pinch the screen to set the deformation. If the camera is unavailable or permission is denied, an illustrated face takes its place. Only the live camera is used: there is no photo-library import.

Camera footage is saved only when you confirm recording. Warp settings and progress are saved on your device. Read the recording explanation, capture up to 15 seconds with the microphone off, then preview the clip before choosing where to share it. Live frames are not automatically uploaded; your chosen sharing destination handles its copy.
