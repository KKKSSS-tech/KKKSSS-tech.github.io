App data

Board choices, progress, results and purchase-state preferences are stored locally. Motion readings are used for the game, not uploaded. No camera, microphone, photos or account is used.

If production ad IDs are configured in a distributed build, the free experience uses Google AdMob. An interstitial ad may appear between rounds, and you may choose to watch a rewarded video for temporary access to the fourth board. Ads are not loaded while a purchase is pending or after the one-time unlock is purchased. A build without production ad IDs does not start the advertising service.

When ads are used, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent before ads load and presents a choice where required. Depending on that choice, iOS may ask for permission to track across other companies' apps and websites. You can decline and continue playing; the advertising identifier is not used for tracking without permission. Where required, you can revisit your choice through the in-app ad privacy settings.

The advertising service may process IP addresses, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic and performance information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Some processing can occur even for non-personalized ads. See How Google uses data. The app does not send game progress or motion readings to the advertising service.

Purchases

StoreKit contacts Apple for products, purchases and restoration. The app uses verified purchase entitlements. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment details. Apple manages transaction records and refunds.

Storage and deletion

To remove local settings and records, delete the app from your device. Keychain credentials, shared copies, device backups and provider records may require separate management.

Public pages and support

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics or external fonts; the host may process access information such as IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your reply address and message are used to handle the inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com for support, privacy questions or requests about inquiry information, including deletion.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
