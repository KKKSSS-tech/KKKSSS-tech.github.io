OverviewPrivacySupport

Use a fold-aware rehearsal desk to keep your script and practice recording close at hand.

Write a script, split it into paragraphs and assign the time for each section. Rehearse with the paragraph timer alone, or prepare the front or back camera and microphone. Tap a retake mark when you want to return to a moment; in history, seek to its video timestamp beside the saved script snapshot.

Wide layouts place manuscript and camera together. On a non-folding iPhone, switch between them in a single column. A compact layout keeps the current paragraph and time in view. There is no automatic speech following or AI scoring.

Free access includes three scripts and playback of the latest video. Pro offers lifetime, monthly or yearly options for more scripts and older video playback. Losing Pro access does not delete your videos. Subscription prices and terms are shown before purchase.

Practice stays on your device. No ads, accounts, cloud sync or sharing.
