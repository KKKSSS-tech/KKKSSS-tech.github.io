ChompJaw

Fold a jaw, time each bite

OverviewPrivacySupport

The fold becomes a monster jaw: close it gently at the right moment to catch a bite.

Feed ChompJaw through six food stages. Watch the monster's preferences, avoid poison, build combos and collect four looks. Timing matters more than motion; there is no reward for snapping the device shut.

Record a gameplay clip when you choose, then share it through the system share sheet.

On a non-folding iPhone, tap to bite. In the free version, full-screen ads may appear automatically at safe breaks, such as after a game or when returning to the app. Rewarded video ads are optional. A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks access.
