TwoWayTalk

A fold between two languages

OverviewPrivacySupport

A folding screen gives two languages room to face each other. TwoWayTalk places the original and translation in separate panels for a nearby conversation.

Type a short phrase or record a short microphone input to transcribe and translate. Keep useful translations in local history and star phrases you want to find again.

Apple's on-device speech and translation features depend on supported languages and downloaded language assets. This is not live call translation; typing remains available when speech input cannot be used.

On a non-folding iPhone, the partner panel rotates to face the other person. No ads, accounts or in-app purchases.
