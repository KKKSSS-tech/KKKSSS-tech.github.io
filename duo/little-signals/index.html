RainRoof

A folded roof, a paper garden

OverviewPrivacySupport

View RainRoof on the App Store (paid app)

Try a folding roof in a virtual paper garden, and see where it shelters the chairs from make-believe rain.

Choose one of three rain directions, adjust the on-screen roof, and seat a rabbit, cat and bear. Explore six arrangement ideas or make a gathering of your own. A grown-up adjusts the model before handing over the chair turn.

There is no score, timer or distressed animal. This is a virtual model, not a weather forecast or a test of device water resistance. Never put real water on the device.

Use the on-screen roof controls on a non-folding iPhone too. Live hardware hinge input is unavailable in this version. The current garden stays locally. No ads, in-app purchases, accounts or external data transmission.
