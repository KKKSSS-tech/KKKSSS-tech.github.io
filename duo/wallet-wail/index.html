A wallet with a dramatic voice

OverviewPrivacySupport

Open a fold-shaped wallet and hear a wordless sigh grow into a theatrical wail.

Your wallet has feelings about being opened. Spread two fingers and hear a sigh, a sob, or a theatrical opera wail. Open wider for a bigger reaction; let go to close. Even with sound muted, the stitched leather face tells the story.

Keep a lighthearted count of today’s openings. Tomorrow starts fresh. No expense entries, budgets or bank accounts.

The sigh and sob are free. Occasional ads appear between openings. An optional rewarded ad gives you one opera opening, or a single purchase unlocks every voice and removes ads. Restore purchases in Voices & settings. Works with a two-finger gesture on a standard phone.
