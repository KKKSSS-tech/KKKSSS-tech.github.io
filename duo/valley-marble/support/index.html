Getting started

Choose a stage and tilt an ordinary phone, or hold the arrow buttons. Resume a saved position after a break. In the 9-by-13 editor, add walls and check that a route reaches the goal before saving. Delete a custom course to remove its file, record and active save. Restore all-stage access in purchase settings.
