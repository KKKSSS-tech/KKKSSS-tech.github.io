Squeezefold

Bellows, chords and loops

OverviewPrivacySupport

A folding bellows becomes a sustained chord: choose the voicing, then let it ring.

Let a chord ring while a quiet backing loop moves beneath it. Choose a four-chord sequence, then spread its notes between two chord faces.

Tap Sustain once to keep the chord sounding. Choose close or open voicing with accessible buttons or a horizontal swipe. Pause keeps your place. No pumping, speed challenge or strong closing is needed.

Home is free. When available, an optional video can unlock Dusk and Drift for the current session; a one-time purchase can remove ads and unlock every sequence. Ads appear only at pauses, never during performance. Music is synthesized locally, with no microphone or music downloads. Touch controls work on ordinary phones.
