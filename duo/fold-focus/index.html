FoldFocus

Close to focus, open to pause

OverviewPrivacySupport

Close the fold to give the next task your attention; open it to pause the focus cycle.

FoldFocus helps students and professionals put their phone aside. Set a duration and press Start. On a folding device, focus and rest advance while closed and pause when opened. Standard devices use proximity and orientation to detect face-down placement. A rotary dial lets you adjust the timer in 1‑minute increments; half‑open angles allow setting concentration periods from 0 to 60 minutes (0 min cannot start). Free users can view daily and the most recent seven days of statistics, while older records stay on the device. Unlock full‑history access with a one‑time purchase. No ads, tracking or accounts are used, and time spent away from the app is not counted. Set your phone down gently and give the next task your attention.
