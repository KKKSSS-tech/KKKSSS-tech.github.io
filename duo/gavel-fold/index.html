GavelFold

Two roles in a tiny court

OverviewPrivacySupport

A folding screen hosts a tiny court with two roles, ready for a verdict on an imaginary object.

GavelFold is a whimsical iOS game that lets you stage tiny fictional trials. Choose between the playful verdicts FORGIVEN or SNACK DUTY and watch a stamp animation seal each case. Four basic charges are free; four extra charges become available after purchasing a one-time unlock that removes all ads, or by watching an optional ad for this session only. You can also create custom fictional charges—up to 120 characters (1024 UTF‑8 bytes) and store up to 20 of them locally. The app shows two role panels on the same phone screen, stacked in narrow layouts, without requiring any special hardware. No camera, account, or cloud sync is used.

Only imaginary objects and silly situations belong here. Never use real people or cases. This is make-believe, not legal advice. Tap to stamp; no slamming or fast folding.

On a non-folding iPhone, the two roles share one screen and the verdict buttons stamp the result.
