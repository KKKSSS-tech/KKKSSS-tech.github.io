App data

Audio, timestamped transcripts and notes stay in the app container on your device. Speech recognition is on-device. The developer does not collect these records, and there are no analytics, advertising or server-based transcription.

There is no advertising SDK. The developer does not collect personal data through the app.

Apple may download speech assets. Export sends Markdown to the file provider you select; the app does not automatically upload meetings. System backups may include recordings and text. Microphone permission can be changed in system settings.

Storage and deletion

Delete a meeting in the archive to remove its local audio, transcript and notes. Removing the app deletes remaining local meetings and settings. Exported Markdown and existing device backups must be managed separately.

Public pages and support

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics or external fonts; the host may process access information such as IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your reply address and message are used to handle the inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com for support, privacy questions or requests about inquiry information, including deletion.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
