FoldPeepers

Eyes that open with a fold

OverviewPrivacySupport

Open the fold, open the eyes. FoldPeepers turns your screen into a pair of expressive peepers.

Change the opening angle to move the eyelids, and tilt the phone to steer the pupils. Choose among five eye styles and let the expressions do the talking. No face recognition, camera or microphone is used.

Not using a folding iPhone? Drag on the screen to move the eyelids and tilt the phone to move the gaze.

The free version includes ads. A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks every eye style. Ads appear away from the eye display, so the expression stays in view while you show it to someone.
