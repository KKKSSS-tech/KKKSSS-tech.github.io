MirrorBloom Garden

Fold reflections into a garden

OverviewPrivacySupport

Change a folding mirror model and watch a small painted motif become a garden of reflections.

MirrorBloom gives you six inks, three motif outlines and six example gardens. Paint, undo or redo, then keep up to twelve gardens locally. The reflections are calculated from a model; your screen does not become a real mirror.

A grown-up can manage saved gardens and optional play time. There is no account, sharing or upload.

On a non-folding iPhone, use the on-screen model angle controls. No ads, in-app purchases, external data transmission, camera or microphone.
