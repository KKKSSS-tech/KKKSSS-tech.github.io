Getting started

Register a recipe with times for its steps, select up to two dishes, and build the plan. Change servings or adjust overlapping tasks before cooking. If notifications are denied, timers remain visible in the app. Deleting a recipe does not remove its existing plan snapshot; rebuild the plan to replace it.
