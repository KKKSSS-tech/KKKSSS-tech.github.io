PrepPace

Two dishes, one cooking plan

OverviewPrivacySupport

Unfold your cooking plan beside your recipes, and make the waiting time in one dish useful for the other.

PrepPace combines up to two of your own recipes into a plan you can adjust by hand. Register ingredients, steps and minutes; change servings to scale quantities; move tasks to make room for parallel preparation.

Follow the plan with step timers. Optional local notifications can tell you when a timer finishes. Recipe and step names may appear in notifications, according to your system settings.

On a non-folding iPhone, switch between Plan and Recipes tabs. Recipes, progress and timers stay on the device. No ads, accounts, cloud sync or in-app purchases.
