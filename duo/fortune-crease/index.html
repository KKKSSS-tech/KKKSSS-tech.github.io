Find today's message at its folding angle. FortuneCrease reveals a small piece of fiction when the hinge reading matches the day's angle.

Keep the daily message and revisit local history. A collection offers more messages, unlocked by a one-time purchase.

On a non-folding iPhone, gently tilt from a flat, face-up position or use the reveal button. Tilt is measured relative to gravity, not a folding angle.

These are invented entertainment messages, not predictions or medical or financial advice. The free daily message and local history remain available without the collection purchase.
