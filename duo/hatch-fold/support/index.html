Getting started

Choose an egg and open it gently. On a non-folding phone, move the touch slider. Each egg has a fixed object; opening speed does not change it. Eight eggs are replayable for free. Restore an all-eggs purchase through the app; the shelf is local and may be included in system backups. Removing the app deletes its local shelf and unfinished shell.
