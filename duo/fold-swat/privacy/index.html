App data

Scores and stage progress stay in local storage. Fold or tap input is processed locally. Optional screen clips are created only after you start recording, then shared only to a destination you choose.

Advertising and choices

Advertising uses Google Mobile Ads and consent management. For ad delivery, measurement and improvement, the SDK may collect IP addresses (which may estimate general location), device or advertising identifiers, crash and performance data, ads viewed and product interactions. See Google's data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Regional consent is checked before ad initialization. Tracking follows your system permission, and you can play without granting it. Personalization is restricted when the necessary consent or tracking permission is absent; this does not eliminate all ad SDK data processing. Revisit consent through the app's advertising privacy options when required, and change tracking permission in system settings. A verified ad-removal purchase stops ad requests. Google manages its retention, deletion and international processing under its policies.

Purchases

StoreKit contacts Apple for products, purchases and restoration. The app uses verified purchase entitlements. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment details. Apple manages transaction records and refunds.

Storage and deletion

Manage local settings and records using the app's deletion controls or by removing the app. Keychain credentials, shared copies, device backups and provider records may require separate management.

Public pages and support

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics or external fonts; the host may process access information such as IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your reply address and message are used to handle the inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com for support, privacy questions or requests about inquiry information, including deletion.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
