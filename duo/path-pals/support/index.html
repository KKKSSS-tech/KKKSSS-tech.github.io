Getting started

Take turns arranging the canyon model and selecting a bridge and landing. Use the virtual model on a non-folding phone. Pause freely and return to saved choices. There is no in-app deletion control; deleting the app removes its local save. The grown-up turn is cooperative, not an authentication check.
