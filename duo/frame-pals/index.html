PaperRig

Paper joints, poses, puppets

OverviewPrivacySupport

Explore folding through a virtual paper joint: build a bird, fish or robot and change its pose on the screen.

PaperRig is a paper-puppet workshop with eight parts, three pose slots and space for twelve saved puppets. Arrange joints with the on-screen controls, keep a pose, then play it back. Work stays on your device.

The current app uses a virtual joint, including on a non-folding iPhone. It does not use live hardware hinge input. A grown-up can manage saved work through the protected controls; the system authentication does not check age.

No ads, in-app purchases, external data transmission, accounts, camera or microphone.
