ToastFold

Tilt toppings onto your toast

OverviewPrivacySupport

A fold becomes illustrated bread: place toppings, tilt gently and finish your sandwich.

A tomato slice, a curl of onion, a golden egg. Make your own little bread picture in ToastFold.

Choose from 30 original illustrated toppings, with 24 available free. Add ingredients and tilt gently to watch them slide across two bread planes. Tap Finish sandwich when you like the arrangement, then share a still image. Your current bread stays saved on your device.

Ordinary phones use device tilt. Completion uses an on-screen button: there is no need to slam or fully close your device, or place real food on it.

A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks all toppings. An optional video can unlock the six extra toppings for the current bread. No subscription. This is a playful illustration app, not a recipe or nutrition tool.
