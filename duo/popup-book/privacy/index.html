App data

Books you create, chosen images, text and reading progress are stored locally. Selected photos are read only when you import them; they are not uploaded by the app. The app does not access the camera, microphone or device location permission.

When production advertising identifiers are configured, the free version may show Google ads between activities and may offer a rewarded ad to try a locked tale. Ads are not shown while reading or making a book. After a purchase is verified, later launches do not start the ad service; a consent or ad screen already open may remain until it is closed. Without production identifiers, ads remain disabled.

Before loading ads, the app checks advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform and, when applicable, requests iOS tracking permission. If consent does not allow ad requests, ads are not loaded and the free features remain available. If tracking is denied, ad requests are non-personalized. Where required, you can revisit advertising choices in Settings. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostic information and consent choices for ad delivery and measurement. If you allow tracking, identifiers may be used to measure or personalize ads across apps and websites. See Google's Privacy Policy for its data practices. The developer does not receive your payment card details or upload your books and photos for advertising.

Optional recordings may include app audio. A clip leaves the device only when you choose a sharing destination, which handles its copy under its own policy. You can remove created books from the shelf.

Purchases

StoreKit contacts Apple for products, purchases and restoration. The app uses verified purchase entitlements. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment details. Apple manages transaction records and refunds.

Storage and deletion

Manage local settings and records using the app's deletion controls or by removing the app. Keychain credentials, shared copies, device backups and provider records may require separate management.

Public pages and support

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics or external fonts; the host may process access information such as IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your reply address and message are used to handle the inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com for support, privacy questions or requests about inquiry information, including deletion.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
