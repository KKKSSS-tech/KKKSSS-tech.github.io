TiltLamp

Fold to dim your lights

OverviewPrivacySupport

Turn a fold into a light dimmer: map the opening angle to the brightness of your chosen lamps.

Connect HomeKit lights or a Philips Hue Bridge on your local network, choose a light or scene, and set the control range that suits your room. A large dial gives you a direct way to adjust brightness before switching to angle control.

On a non-folding iPhone, use the on-screen dial or device tilt. Compatible lighting is required; TiltLamp controls lights, not other household appliances. Hue pairing and HomeKit access are managed on your device.

No ads or in-app purchases.
