A fold-aware clock for the bedside: large time first, a little context when you want it.

A quiet clock for your bedside. See the time in large, clear type, with local weather and your next calendar event close by. Digital and Serif faces are free; unlock Copper with a $2.99 one-time purchase. Use an ordinary phone sideways while charging. Night dimming and a keep-awake option make it easy to leave the clock on display. Weather and calendar access are optional: the clock works without either permission. Requires iOS 17 or later. No alarms. No ads.
