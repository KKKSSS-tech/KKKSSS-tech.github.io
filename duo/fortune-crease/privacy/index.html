App data

The daily selection, opening state, timezone choice and up to 365 history entries are stored locally. Gravity and hinge readings are used only for revealing the message and are not uploaded. There is no camera, microphone, account or export.

Advertising

When live advertising IDs are not configured, the app does not start advertising consent management or request or display ads. When live IDs are configured, after a free user opens the daily message, the app checks advertising consent and may show a consent form and a tracking permission request. Within the permissions granted, it may initialize Google Mobile Ads and load full-screen ads at natural breaks and optional rewarded video ads. Depending on consent and device settings, the advertising service may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics. Ads are not shown to users with a verified one-time purchase.

Purchases

StoreKit contacts Apple for products, purchases and restoration. The app uses verified purchase entitlements. The developer does not receive card numbers or payment details. Apple manages transaction records and refunds.

Storage and deletion

Manage local settings and records using the app's deletion controls or by removing the app. Keychain credentials, shared copies, device backups and provider records may require separate management.

Public pages and support

These pages use GitHub Pages. We add no analytics or external fonts; the host may process access information such as IP addresses. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

If you email support, your reply address and message are used to handle the inquiry. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information. Contact kkksss.support@icloud.com for support, privacy questions or requests about inquiry information, including deletion.

Changes to data handling will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
