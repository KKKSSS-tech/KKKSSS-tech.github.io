Unfold a porcelain discovery

OverviewPrivacySupport

Gently unfold a porcelain shell and discover the object waiting inside.

Spread a porcelain shell and discover a tiny star, a seashell, a fern or a teapot. HatchFold has 24 fixed little wonders to collect: no random draws, rarity tiers or speed challenges. Open gently with the touch slider and keep your discoveries on a quiet specimen shelf. Eight eggs can be opened again and again for free. Optional reward videos let you hatch a selected extra egg once. A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks every egg. Your shelf and unfinished shell stay on this device. Purchases can be restored through the App Store. Free play includes ads only at natural breaks. The touch slider works on ordinary phones; opening faster never gives better results.
