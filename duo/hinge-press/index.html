FlatCrush

Fold to press a photo cutout

OverviewPrivacySupport

The fold becomes a picture press: gently close to flatten a cutout of an object.

Choose an object photo and bring it to the press. FlatCrush cuts out the foreground so you can squash it, crack it and collect the result.

Hard objects resist before cracking. Soft objects flatten smoothly. Bouncy objects compress with a springy feel. Each material has its own sound.

Press and hold on a standard screen. Record up to 15 seconds of the action, review your clip, then share it using the system share sheet. Your flattened objects stay in a local collection until you delete them.

Use object photos only. Photos with detected faces, people or supported animals are rejected; automatic detection is not perfect.

All three materials, recording and the collection are free. The free version includes ads. An optional rewarded video adds a golden crack to the next press. A one-time full unlock removes ads and includes the golden effect for every press.
