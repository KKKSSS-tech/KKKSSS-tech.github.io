BlinkFirst

Who will laugh first?

OverviewPrivacySupport

Set a fold-aware face-off between two friends and see who laughs first.

BlinkFirst is a local laughing game. Start a round, try an optional silly-face prompt and hold your expression. The camera can estimate mouth shape on the device, but its estimate is only a game cue. Use the always-available buttons to decide who laughed when the camera misses the moment.

A round ends in a draw after 45 seconds. First to two wins is free; the one-time unlock removes ads and adds first-to-three matches. Ads may appear when leaving the completed match, never during a round or between rounds.

On a non-folding iPhone, or when a second camera is unavailable, use the same manual result buttons. You can play without camera permission. Dual camera operation depends on the device.

Camera analysis does not save frames or send them to a developer server. An optional clip requires your recording confirmation, lasts up to 15 seconds with the microphone off, and can be reviewed before you choose a sharing destination.
