Getting started

Create a trip, add a dated item and import one document at a time from Files (up to 15 MB each). Keep originals outside the app. Confirm deletion to remove a document, plan or trip; there is no undo or archive recovery. StoreKit handles Pro only, not travel bookings. Restore Pro in the app.
